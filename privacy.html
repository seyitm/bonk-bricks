We collect no data

Bonk: Bricks does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The game works entirely offline and makes no network connections.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The only things the game keeps are your best score and your settings (language, music and sound volume). They are stored on your own device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
